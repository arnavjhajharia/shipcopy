App Store "What's New" from a changelog

You already have the facts in git. The store wants benefit-first bullets, under 4000 characters, with no raw ticket IDs unless your users care.

Manual recipe (5 minutes)

  1. Export commits since your last tag: git log v1.2.0..HEAD --oneline
  2. Group by user-visible theme (fixes, new feature, performance).
  3. Rewrite each line as "You can now…" or "Fixed…" — drop merge commits and refactors.
  4. Paste into App Store Connect → What's New. Keep Play Store "Release notes" in sync (similar length).

Example output shape

• Dark mode follows your system setting
• Faster startup on older phones
• Fixed crash when signing out offline
